# Hardware Asset Management

The **Hardware Asset Management (HAM)** sub-module manages physical infrastructure:

- **Lifecycle Tracking**: Serial numbers, MAC addresses, asset tags, CPU, RAM, disk specs, and physical locations.
- **Warranty Integration**: Connects to Dell, HP, and Lenovo APIs to retrieve real-time manufacturer warranty expiration dates.
- **Depot Management**: Monitors spare pool inventory levels to avoid equipment shortages.
- **Mobile Audits**: Use the NuVision mobile app to scan barcodes and QR codes during physical floor audits.
