# Asset Lifecycle States

NuVision tracks hardware and virtual assets through their entire lifecycle:

```
[Requested] -> [Ordered] -> [In Stock] -> [Deployed] -> [Maintenance] -> [Retired]
```

- **Requested**: Procurement or provisioning request submitted.
- **Ordered**: Purchase order placed with vendor.
- **In Stock**: Received at warehouse/IT depot and available for assignment.
- **Deployed**: Actively assigned to a user, system, or data center rack.
- **Maintenance**: Offline for repairs, upgrades, or reconfiguration.
- **Retired**: Safely decommissioned, sanitized, and disposed of.
