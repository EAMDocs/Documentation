# Roadmap Planning

The interactive roadmap planner lets teams schedule and communicate multi-quarter delivery schedules:

1. **Swimlane Grouping**: Group roadmap items by Business Unit, Capability, or Technical Domain.
2. **Milestone Scheduling**: Drag and drop initiative cards across time horizons (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4).
3. **Dependency Mapping**: Visualize cross-project blockers and prerequisite relationships.
4. **Executive Presentations**: Export polished timeline views directly to PDF or PowerPoint.
