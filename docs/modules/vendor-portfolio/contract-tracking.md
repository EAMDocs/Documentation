# Contract Tracking

Avoid surprise evergreen auto-renewals with proactive contract governance:

- **Key Contract Dates**: Effective date, expiration date, notice-of-cancellation deadline.
- **Terms & Types**: Master Services Agreement (MSA), Statement of Work (SOW), SLA, EULA, NDA.
- **Renewal Alerts**: Automated notices dispatched 90, 60, and 30 days prior to non-renewal windows.
- **Document Repository**: Store signed PDFs and amendments directly on the contract record.
