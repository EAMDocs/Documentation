# Rationalisation Scoring

NuVision uses the industry-standard **TIME Model** to categorize applications based on Business Value vs. Technical Quality:

| Quadrant | Criteria | Recommended Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tolerate** | High Technical Quality, Low Business Value | Maintain as-is with minimal investment |
| **Invest** | High Technical Quality, High Business Value | Allocate funding to enhance and scale |
| **Migrate** | Low Technical Quality, High Business Value | Re-platform, modernize, or replace |
| **Eliminate** | Low Technical Quality, Low Business Value | Decommission and migrate remaining users |
