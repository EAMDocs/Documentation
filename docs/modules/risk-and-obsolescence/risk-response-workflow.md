# Risk Response Workflow

When an asset exceeds your organization's risk tolerance threshold:

1. **Auto-Ticket Creation**: An incident or risk item is generated and routed to the asset owner.
2. **Mitigation Strategy Selection**: Choose from Accept, Mitigate, Transfer, or Avoid.
3. **Action Item Tracking**: Assign upgrade, re-platforming, or decommissioning tasks.
4. **Audit History**: All remediation notes, sign-offs, and risk acceptance waivers are logged for compliance reviews.
