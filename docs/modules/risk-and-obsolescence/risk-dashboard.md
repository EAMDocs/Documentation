# Risk Dashboard

The Risk Dashboard provides a high-level view of your enterprise technology exposure:

- **Risk Heatmap**: Visual 2D matrix mapping vulnerability Likelihood vs. Business Impact.
- **Upcoming EOL Milestones**: Timeline of operating systems, databases, and packages reaching end-of-support over 3, 6, 12, and 24-month horizons.
- **Critical CVE Tracker**: Software inventory continuously matched against the NIST National Vulnerability Database (NVD).
