# EOL and EOS Tracking

NuVision maintains a curated catalog of over **5,000+ technology products** with verified vendor lifecycle dates:

- **End-of-Life (EOL)**: Product is no longer manufactured or sold.
- **End-of-Support (EOS)**: Vendor ceases security patches, bug fixes, and technical support.
- **Automated Notifications**: Configurable email and Slack/Teams alerts at 180, 90, and 30-day thresholds prior to vendor deadlines.
