1.1  What is NuVision?
----------------------
NuVision is PEMVISH's flagship SaaS platform. It acts as a unified command
centre combining:

  - Enterprise Architecture Management (EAM)
  - Application Portfolio Management (APM) & App Rationalization
  - IT Asset Management (ITAM)
  - Software Asset Management (SAM)
  - Hardware Asset Management (HAM)
  - Technology Risk & Obsolescence Management
  - Business Portfolio & Roadmap Planning
  - Vendor Portfolio Management

Rather than requiring heavy traditional customisation, NuVision uses
configurable data models and GUI workflows to adapt to an organisation's
specific processes and terminology.


1.2  Core Design Principles
----------------------------
  CONFIGURATION-DRIVEN   No custom code required. Everything from data models
                         to workflows is configured through the GUI.

  WHITE-GLOVE ONBOARDING PEMVISH engineers handle initial setup, field mapping,
                         and integration configuration. Customers go live in
                         weeks, not months.

  ZERO-CODE INTEGRATION  Pre-built connectors link NuVision to existing
                         enterprise tools (ITSM, HR platforms, cloud billing)
                         via API — no scripting required.

  SOC 2 COMPLIANT        All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit
                         (TLS 1.3). Serverless SaaS — no infrastructure
                         provisioning required.

  Data Security           AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit

CHAPTER 2: GETTING STARTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1  Prerequisites
-------------------
Before logging in to NuVision, ensure you have:

  [ ] A valid PEMVISH-provisioned tenant URL
      Format: https://yourorg.nuvision.pemvish.com

  [ ] A corporate email address approved by your IT administrator

  [ ] A modern web browser:
        - Google Chrome 110 or later
        - Microsoft Edge 110 or later
        - Mozilla Firefox 115 or later
        - Apple Safari 16 or later

  [ ] Minimum screen resolution: 1280 x 768 pixels


2.2  Account Activation
------------------------
Step 1  Check your corporate inbox for an email from noreply@pemvish.com
        Subject: "Your NuVision Account is Ready"

Step 2  Click the [Activate Account] button in the email.
        NOTE: Activation links expire after 72 hours.

Step 3  Set a strong password meeting the following requirements:
          - Minimum 12 characters
          - At least one uppercase letter
          - At least one number
          - At least one special character (e.g., @, #, $, !)

Step 4  Enrol in Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
        Supported authenticator apps:
          - Google Authenticator
          - Microsoft Authenticator
          - Authy
          - Any TOTP-compatible app

Step 5  Accept the platform Terms of Use to complete activation.

IMPORTANT: If your activation link has expired, ask your NuVision Administrator
to resend the invitation from: Admin → Users → [Your Name] → Resend Invite


2.3  First Login
-----------------
Step 1  Navigate to your tenant URL in your browser.
Step 2  Enter your email address and password.
Step 3  Complete the MFA challenge (enter the 6-digit code from your app).
Step 4  On first login, the Onboarding Wizard launches automatically.
        Follow the guided steps to configure your organisation's basic profile.


2.4  Default User Roles
------------------------
  ROLE              DESCRIPTION                     DEFAULT PERMISSIONS
  ----------------- ------------------------------- --------------------------
  Super Admin       Full platform access            All modules, users, billing
  Platform Admin    Module configuration            All modules, no billing
  Contributor       Create & edit records           Assigned modules, no admin
  Viewer            Read-only access                Assigned modules, read-only

NOTE: Custom roles with granular module-level permissions can be configured
by a Super Admin or Platform Admin.

CHAPTER 3: ENTERPRISE ARCHITECTURE MANAGEMENT (EAM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1  Overview
--------------
The EAM module enables architects and business analysts to build a living
blueprint of your organisation's business capabilities, application landscape,
data flows, and technology strategy.

All diagrams are stored as structured data — not static images — so they stay
up-to-date as your environment evolves.


3.2  Architecture Canvas
-------------------------
The Canvas is the primary workspace for building and visualising architecture
models.

  KEY FEATURES:
  - Drag-and-drop modelling from the left-hand palette
  - Layered views: Business, Application, Technology, Data
  - Auto-layout: hierarchical or force-directed with one click
  - Export: SVG, PNG, PDF, or live embeddable link (Confluence/SharePoint)
  - Version history with compare and rollback


3.3  Business Capability Mapping
----------------------------------
Step 1  Navigate to: EAM → Capability Map
Step 2  Click [+ New Capability] and enter:
          - Capability name
          - Description
          - Business owner
Step 3  Drag child capabilities onto the canvas to build a hierarchy.
Step 4  Colour-code capabilities using the Heatmap toolbar:
          - By maturity level
          - By investment level
          - By strategic priority
Step 5  Link capabilities to applications, processes, and business units
        using the Relationship Editor.

BEST PRACTICE: Start with Level-1 capabilities (5–10 top-level domains)
before drilling into sub-capabilities. This prevents scope creep and
aligns the model with executive reporting.


3.4  Architecture Roadmap
--------------------------
  VIEW              PURPOSE
  ----------------- -------------------------------------------------------
  Current State     Snapshot of today's architecture baseline
  Target State      Desired future-state architecture
  Transition Plan   Phased milestones bridging current → target state
  Gap Analysis      Auto-highlights capability gaps and redundancies


3.5  Supported Viewpoints
--------------------------
  - Business Architecture View
  - Application Architecture View
  - Technology Architecture View
  - Data Architecture View
  - Security Architecture View
  - Integration Architecture View

CHAPTER 4: APPLICATION PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT (APM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1  Overview
--------------
The APM module gives your organisation a single source of truth for every
business application — from mission-critical ERP systems to departmental
shadow-IT tools.


4.2  Application Inventory Fields
-----------------------------------
Each application record captures:

  FIELD                   DESCRIPTION
  ----------------------- ------------------------------------------------
  Application Name        Official name of the application
  Description             Brief functional description
  Business Owner          Business department / person responsible
  IT Owner                Technical team responsible
  Hosting Model           On-premise / Cloud-IaaS / SaaS / Hybrid
  Vendor                  Software vendor name
  Version                 Current deployed version
  License Type            Perpetual / Subscription / Open Source
  Business Capabilities   Linked EAM capability map entries
  Integrations            Systems this application connects to
  Annual Cost             License + Infrastructure + Support (USD)
  EOL Date                Vendor-published End-of-Life date
  EOS Date                Vendor-published End-of-Support date


4.3  Rationalisation Scoring (TIME Model)
------------------------------------------
NuVision calculates a composite TIME Score for each application:

  TOLERATE   Moderate business value, adequate technical health.
             Action: Maintain as-is; low investment priority.

  INVEST     High business value, strong technical health.
             Action: Enhance or expand capabilities.

  MIGRATE    High business value, poor technical health.
             Action: Replace with modern alternative or consolidate.

  ELIMINATE  Low business value, poor technical health.
             Action: Decommission; migrate users to other tools.

Scoring weights are configurable per organisation from:
  Settings → APM → Rationalisation → Scoring Model


4.4  Bulk Import
-----------------
Step 1  Navigate to: APM → Import → Download Template
Step 2  Populate the Excel or CSV template with your application data.
Step 3  Upload the file. The system validates and flags:
          - Duplicate records
          - Missing mandatory fields
          - Invalid date formats
Step 4  Review the import preview.
Step 5  Click [Confirm Import] to finalise.

TIP: Connect NuVision to your ITSM (ServiceNow, Jira) via the
Zero-Code Integration Pipeline to auto-populate inventory without
manual CSV uploads. See Chapter 9.

CHAPTER 5: IT ASSET MANAGEMENT (ITAM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1  Overview
--------------
The ITAM module provides a unified, real-time inventory of all physical and
virtual assets across your enterprise — workstations, servers, cloud instances,
and network devices.


5.2  Asset Discovery Methods
-----------------------------
  METHOD                  DESCRIPTION
  ----------------------- ------------------------------------------------
  Agent-based             Lightweight NuVision Discovery Agent (Windows,
                          macOS, Linux) — most accurate, real-time
  Agentless               WMI, SSH, and SNMP scans from a discovery server
  Cloud-native            Direct API connectors (AWS, Azure, GCP)
  CMDB Sync               Bi-directional sync with ServiceNow, Jira Assets,
                          BMC Helix


5.3  Asset Lifecycle States
-----------------------------
  STATE               DESCRIPTION
  ------------------- -------------------------------------------------------
  Requested           Asset purchase/provisioning has been requested
  Ordered             Purchase order raised with vendor
  In Stock            Received but not yet assigned
  Deployed            Actively assigned to a user or system
  Under Maintenance   Temporarily offline for repair or upgrade
  Retired             Decommissioned; removed from active service


5.4  Ghost Asset Detection
---------------------------
NuVision automatically flags "ghost assets" — assets present in inventory
records but not detected on the network for more than 30 days (configurable).

  To review ghost assets:
    Navigate to: ITAM → Reconciliation → Ghost Assets

  Actions available per ghost asset:
    - Confirm as Retired
    - Trigger manual rediscovery scan
    - Transfer to different network segment
    - Raise a support ticket


5a. SOFTWARE ASSET MANAGEMENT (SAM)
-------------------------------------
Embedded within ITAM, the SAM sub-module:

  - Automatically discovers locally installed applications via the Discovery
    Agent or agentless WMI/SSH scan
  - Maps discovered software to license entitlements
  - Calculates real-time compliance status (over-licensed / under-licensed)
  - Tracks per-seat, volume, subscription, and concurrent-use license models
  - Monitors software warranty and support contract expiry dates
  - Generates audit-ready compliance reports for:
      Microsoft, Oracle, SAP, Adobe, IBM, Citrix


5b. HARDWARE ASSET MANAGEMENT (HAM)
--------------------------------------
  - Tracks make, model, serial number, and warranty status
  - Integrates with vendor warranty APIs (Dell, HP, Lenovo) for real-time
    warranty status checks
  - Generates End-of-Warranty alerts at 90, 60, and 30 days before expiry
  - Supports barcode / QR code scanning for physical asset audits via the
    NuVision Mobile App (iOS and Android)

CHAPTER 6: TECHNOLOGY RISK & OBSOLESCENCE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1  Overview
--------------
This module proactively monitors your technology portfolio for risks — including
EOL/EOS dates, known CVEs, vendor stability risks, and infrastructure health
degradation — before they become business-impacting incidents.


6.2  Risk Dashboard Components
--------------------------------
  COMPONENT            DESCRIPTION
  -------------------- -------------------------------------------------------
  Risk Heatmap         Colour-coded matrix: Likelihood × Impact
  EOL/EOS Timeline     Gantt-style view of upcoming end-of-support dates
                       across the next 24 months
  CVE Feed             NIST NVD feed matched against installed software versions
  Risk Trend Chart     Week-over-week risk score evolution


6.3  EOL / EOS Tracking
------------------------
Step 1  NuVision maintains a curated EOL database covering 5,000+ products
        (operating systems, databases, middleware, hardware).

Step 2  As your asset and application records are populated, the system
        automatically cross-references EOL dates.

Step 3  Configurable alert thresholds trigger notifications at:
          - 180 days before EOL
          -  90 days before EOL
          -  30 days before EOL

Step 4  Each EOL item generates a Risk Record with recommended actions:
          - Upgrade to supported version
          - Migrate to alternative product
          - Replace hardware / decommission


6.4  Risk Scoring Model (Default Weights)
------------------------------------------
  FACTOR                              DEFAULT WEIGHT   DATA SOURCE
  ----------------------------------- ---------------- -----------------------
  EOL / EOS proximity                 30%              PEMVISH EOL database
  CVE severity (CVSS score)           25%              NIST NVD
  Business criticality of dependents  20%              APM module
  Asset coverage & redundancy         15%              ITAM module
  Vendor stability                    10%              Manual assessment

NOTE: Weights are fully configurable per organisation.
  Navigate to: Settings → Risk Configuration → Scoring Model


6.5  Risk Response Workflow
----------------------------
  1. Risk identified → Risk Record auto-created
  2. Risk owner assigned (from org chart or manual selection)
  3. Response plan documented (Accept / Mitigate / Transfer / Avoid)
  4. Mitigation tasks linked to roadmap activities (EAM)
  5. Risk closed when mitigation is verified

9.1  Overview

9.2  Supported Integrations

  Monitoring      Datadog                             Read (asset health)
                  Splunk                              Write (audit log export)

9.3  Setting Up a Connector
-----------------------------
Step 1  Navigate to: Settings → Integrations → Connector Library
Step 2  Select the target system from the catalogue.
Step 3  Click [Configure] and enter credentials:
          - API key
          - OAuth 2.0 tokens
          - Service account credentials
Step 4  Define:
          - Sync scope (which data entities to sync)
          - Field mappings (source field → NuVision field)
          - Sync frequency (Real-time webhook / Hourly / Daily)
Step 5  Click [Test Connection] to validate connectivity.
Step 6  Click [Activate] to enable the connector.


9.4  REST API
--------------
Every NuVision data entity is accessible via the NuVision REST API.

  Specification:  OpenAPI 3.0
  Authentication: Bearer Token (configurable scopes)
  Download spec:  Settings → API → Download OpenAPI Spec
  Base URL:       https://api.nuvision.pemvish.com/v1/

  COMMON ENDPOINTS:
    GET    /assets              List all assets
    POST   /assets              Create a new asset
    GET    /assets/{id}         Get asset by ID
    PUT    /assets/{id}         Update asset
    DELETE /assets/{id}         Delete asset
    GET    /applications        List all applications
    GET    /risks               List risk records
    GET    /reports/{id}/export Export report as CSV/JSON

Step 3  Enter the user's email address.

10.2  Single Sign-On (SSO) Configuration
-----------------------------------------
NuVision supports SAML 2.0 and OIDC:

Step 1  Navigate to: Admin → Security → SSO
Step 2  Download the NuVision SAML metadata XML.
Step 3  Upload the metadata XML to your Identity Provider (Okta, Azure AD,
        Ping Identity, ADFS).
Step 4  Paste your IdP metadata URL or XML into NuVision.
Step 5  Click [Save].
Step 6  Test SSO with a non-admin user before enforcing org-wide.
Step 7  Navigate to: Admin → Security → SSO → Enforce SSO
        Toggle enforcement on after successful testing.


10.3  Audit Logs
-----------------
Every user action is captured in tamper-evident audit logs.

  RETENTION:   12 months (default) — configurable up to 7 years
  FILTERS:     User, date range, action type, module
  EXPORT:      CSV or JSON
  SIEM:        Webhook to Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel, Datadog, Elastic

  HIGH-RISK ALERTS:
  Set up real-time webhook alerts for:
    - Bulk record deletions
    - Role escalation events
    - Admin-level configuration changes
    - API key creation / revocation


10.4  Backup & Data Export
---------------------------
Full data export available at any time:
  Navigate to: Admin → Data Management → Full Export

Export format: ZIP archive containing:
  - JSON files for all entity types
  - CSV files for tabular data
  - Attachment index (links to stored files)

Export frequency options:
  - On-demand
  - Scheduled (daily / weekly)
  - Triggered via API

12.3  Common Issues & Solutions
---------------------------------

ISSUE: Cannot log in — activation link has expired.
SOLUTION:
  Ask your NuVision Administrator to resend the invitation:
    Admin → Users → [Your Name] → Resend Invite

ISSUE: Discovery Agent is not reporting assets.
SOLUTION:
  1. Check the agent service is running:
       Windows:  sc query NuVisionAgent
       Linux:    systemctl status nuvision-agent
  2. Ensure outbound HTTPS (port 443) is allowed to *.nuvision.pemvish.com
  3. Review agent logs:
       Windows:  %ProgramData%\NuVision\logs\agent.log
       Linux:    /var/log/nuvision/agent.log
  4. Restart the agent service and wait 5 minutes for the first heartbeat.

ISSUE: MFA device lost or changed.
SOLUTION:
  An Administrator resets MFA from:
    Admin → Users → [User Name] → Reset MFA
  The user will be prompted to re-enrol on next login.

ISSUE: SSO login fails with "SAML assertion invalid".
SOLUTION:
  1. Verify system clocks on the IdP and NuVision are synchronised (NTP).
  2. Ensure the SAML signing certificate has not expired.
  3. Confirm the SP entity ID in your IdP matches the value in NuVision
     (Admin → Security → SSO → Service Provider Details).

ISSUE: Connector sync failing with 401 Unauthorized.
SOLUTION:
  1. Navigate to Settings → Integrations → [Connector] → Credentials.
  2. Re-enter the API key or refresh the OAuth token.
  3. Verify the service account has not had its permissions changed.
  4. Click [Test Connection] to validate before reactivating.

CHAPTER 13: GLOSSARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  APM          Application Portfolio Management — the practice of managing
               the full lifecycle of business applications.

  CMDB         Configuration Management Database — a repository of
               information about IT assets and their relationships.

  EAM          Enterprise Architecture Management — the discipline of
               aligning business strategy with IT systems and processes.

  EOL          End of Life — the date after which a vendor no longer sells
               or manufactures a product.

  EOS          End of Support — the date after which a vendor no longer
               provides patches, updates, or technical support.

  Ghost Asset  An asset that exists in inventory records but is no longer
               detected on the network for more than the configured threshold.

  HAM          Hardware Asset Management — tracking and managing physical IT
               hardware throughout its lifecycle.

  ITAM         IT Asset Management — the set of business practices joining
               financial, contractual, and inventory functions for IT assets.

  MFA          Multi-Factor Authentication — a security mechanism requiring
               two or more verification factors to access an account.

  NuVision     PEMVISH's flagship SaaS platform for enterprise technology
               and architecture governance.

  PEMVISH      Princeton-based enterprise software company (est. 2022)
               specialising in configuration-driven enterprise architecture
               and IT asset management.

  SAM          Software Asset Management — the management of software
               licenses, compliance, and usage optimisation.

  SOC 2        Service Organisation Control 2 — an auditing standard for
               SaaS providers covering security, availability, and
               confidentiality controls.

  TIME Model   Tolerate, Invest, Migrate, Eliminate — a framework for
               rationalising an application portfolio.

  Zero-Code    PEMVISH's configuration-driven connector pipeline that links
  Integration  NuVision to external tools without requiring custom code.
