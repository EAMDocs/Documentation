# Support Channels

Connect with the PEMVISH support team through your preferred channel:

- **In-App Live Chat**: Available 24/5 (Monday through Friday) directly in the bottom right corner of the platform.
- **Email Support**: `support@pemvish.com` (Monitored 24/7).
- **Customer Support Portal**: `https://support.pemvish.com` for ticket management and knowledgebase.
- **Emergency P1 Hotline**: 24/7 telephone access for critical platform outages.
