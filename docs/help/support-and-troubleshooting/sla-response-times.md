# SLA Response Times

Our committed Service Level Agreements (SLAs) based on ticket severity:

| Severity | Definition | Initial Response | Resolution Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P1 Critical** | Platform down, critical data impact | 15 Minutes | 4 Hours |
| **P2 High** | Core module degraded, no workaround | 2 Hours | 24 Hours |
| **P3 Medium** | Feature issue, workaround available | 8 Hours | 72 Hours |
| **P4 Low** | General query, minor cosmetic bug | 2 Business Days | Next Release |
