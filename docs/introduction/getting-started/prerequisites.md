# Prerequisites

Before accessing NuVision, ensure you meet the following baseline requirements:

1. **Tenant URL**: A provisioned NuVision domain provided by your administrator (`https://<org>.nuvision.pemvish.com`).
2. **Approved Corporate Email**: Accounts must match your organization's approved email domain.
3. **Supported Browsers**:
   - Google Chrome (version 110 or higher)
   - Microsoft Edge (version 110 or higher)
   - Mozilla Firefox (version 115 or higher)
   - Apple Safari (version 16 or higher)
4. **Display**: Recommended minimum screen resolution of 1280 × 768.
